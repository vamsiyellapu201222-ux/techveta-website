Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

TechVeta Automation is a private tool operated by the owner of the TechVeta Telugu YouTube channel. It is used only to prepare and manage that channel's video publishing workflow.

Information and access

When the channel owner connects a Google account, the app requests YouTube permissions needed to view the authorized channel and upload videos. It may process video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and scheduled publishing details supplied by the channel owner.

Use of information

Authorized YouTube data is used only to operate the channel owner's content workflow, including uploading and scheduling videos when enabled by the owner. The app does not sell personal information or use YouTube data for advertising.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and workflow configuration are stored in access-controlled GitHub repository secrets and used by the owner's automated workflow. Video and workflow output files may be stored as GitHub Actions artifacts subject to GitHub retention settings. Data is not shared except with service providers needed to operate the workflow, such as Google/YouTube and GitHub.

Retention and deletion

The channel owner can revoke Google access from Google Account security settings and delete workflow credentials and artifacts from the relevant GitHub settings. YouTube data is retained only as needed to operate the workflow or as otherwise retained by Google/YouTube.

Security

The app is intended for its owner. Credentials should be kept private and stored only in protected secret storage. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Contact

For questions, contact the TechVeta Telugu channel owner through the official YouTube channel.
